The seeds of our collective were cast into Nocturnal Pest many years ago. The members of two friend groups found each other, almost as if predestined, treading the dance floor of steamy clubs, dancing to hypnotically pulsating beats of machine-music, or philosophizing in house parties til dawn. Eventually, on the surreal, purple-glowing deck of a Berlin club boat, was born the idea: it is time to organize! We threw our first firewood on the ever-burning flame of Nocturnal Pest in January of 2022 and since then we have visited – either as DJs or as organisers – the prominent underground venues of Budapest.
